\paragraph{S5 result.}
Neither calibration rate passes the original sequence-ratio gate, so the lower pre-specified fallback rate \(10^{-6}\) is used. At that rate the selected-token clip fraction is below \(1\%\) for every arm and the sign-aware token saturation fraction is about \(0.4\%\), including K1; the original sequence-product gate is therefore retained as the registered decision rule but is not interpreted as a token-level clipping rate. At H8, \Eone{}--\Ezero{} is \(+1.25\) points (\(95\%\) CI \([-0.50,+3.25]\)) and \Ethree{}--\Ezero{} is \(+0.75\) (\([-1.00,+2.50]\)); at H16 they are \(0.00\) (\([-1.75,+1.75]\)) and \(+1.00\) (\([-1.00,+3.00]\)). H16 raises accuracy by \(17.75\)--\(19.25\) points and answer rate by \(21.50\)--\(23.00\) points across arms, but does not reveal a resolved correction advantage.
